### FAQ: Before I start You might have question why we learning fundamentals instead of Learning Deep Learning model?

* Using the some aritechture of deep learning models without understanding the underlying principles can limit your ability to innovate and adapt to new problems.

* If your deep learing model fails, understanding the fundamentals will help you diagnose the problem and make necessary adjustments effectively.

* While you train model you need dataset to learn from, understanding the fundamentals will help you better prepare and utilize the data effectively.

* Curiosity is naturally satisfied when you understand the history and fundamentals, evolution of the field, as it encourages you to explore and ask deeper questions about how and why things work.
